Create a local clone of your course GitLab repo from https://gitlab.cs.uchicago.edu/courses/aut-26/mpcs52553.

Create a new directory at the repository root called lab-1. Follow the directions in README.md in the lab-1 directory in the course GitHub: https://github.com/UChicagoWebDev/course_materials/assignments/lab-1

Commit and push your changes. When you are done, submit the URL for your GitLab directory here on Canvas. That signals to the instructors that your assignment is ready to be graded. In the event of a conflict, GitLab timestamps are canonical, so you are not penalized for forgetting to submit on Canvas, but it may delay how quickly your work can be reviewed.
