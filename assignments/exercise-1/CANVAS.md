Create a new directory in your repository root called exercise-1. Follow the directions in README.md in the exercise-1 directory in the course GitHub: https://github.com/UChicagoWebDev/course_materials/assignments/exercise-1

Commit and push your changes. When you are done, submit the URL for your GitLab directory here on Canvas. That signals to the instructors that your assignment is ready to be graded.
